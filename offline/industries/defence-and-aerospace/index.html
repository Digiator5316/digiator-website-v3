Protective research and technical software

Support research with clearer information and review.

Our work in this area is at prototype and active-research stage. The focus is simulation-led protective analysis, research organisation and decision-support software, with people responsible for interpreting outputs and making decisions.

For Research teams and organisations exploring protective technical workflows.

What can we discuss with Digiator at the research stage?

A bounded protective-research workflow is an appropriate starting point: organising approved technical material, presenting analysis or recording human review. We can explore knowledge interfaces and decision-support prototypes around agreed access and evaluation requirements. This capability remains at prototype and active-research stage, with people interpreting outputs and making decisions.
