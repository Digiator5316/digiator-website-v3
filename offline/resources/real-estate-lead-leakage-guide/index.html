Before you start +

Choose one project and a recent enquiry cohort, allowing enough time for follow-up. Work with marketing and sales together. Record aggregate counts and anonymised process examples; do not include prospect names, phone numbers or financial information.

Use aggregate or fictional information. Leave out personal and confidential details.
