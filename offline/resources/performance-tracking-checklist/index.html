Before you start +

Review one campaign and one reporting period. Complete this with whoever manages campaigns and whoever receives enquiries. Use aggregate counts and test records only. Keep personal details, account credentials and advertising identifiers out of the worksheet.

Use aggregate or fictional information. Leave out personal and confidential details.
