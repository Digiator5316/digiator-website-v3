Website terms

The basis for using this website and its resources.

About this website

This website introduces Digiator’s capabilities and provides general project-planning resources. Browsing the site or sending an enquiry does not establish a paid engagement.

Project agreements

The scope, deliverables, fees, timeline, access, support and ownership terms for a project are set out in the relevant written agreement. General website descriptions do not replace that agreement.

Case studies and illustrations

Case-study results relate to specific engagements. Owner-confirmed results and estimates are identified where used. They are not guarantees for a future project. Workflow illustrations are explanatory diagrams rather than representations of a client’s actual interface.

Using the resources

You may use the workbooks for your own business planning and share completed copies with your project team. They provide prompts for investigation, not a professional certification, legal opinion or guarantee about an outcome.

Appropriate use

Do not misuse the website, attempt unauthorised access, or send confidential third-party information through an initial enquiry. Discuss an appropriate information-sharing arrangement for sensitive project material.

Contact

For questions about the website or these terms, contact operations@digiator.com. Last updated: 22 October 2026.
