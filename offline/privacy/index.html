Privacy information

How this website handles the information you choose to share.

Who to contact

Digiator — Website Development and Digital Marketing Agency operates from Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India. For questions about website information or an enquiry, contact operations@digiator.com.

Enquiry details

The enquiry form prepares a message on your device. It does not send the form to a Digiator website database. Choosing WhatsApp or email opens the selected service; you must complete the send there. Once you send the message, Digiator receives the information included in it and can use it to respond and discuss your project.

WhatsApp and email

Your chosen communication service processes messages under its own terms and privacy practices. Share only what is needed for the initial conversation. Do not include patient records, customer lists, passwords or other sensitive project material.

Workbook notes

Text you enter in a workbook stays in the current browser tab. This website does not transmit those notes or save them in browser storage. Download or print your notes before closing the tab. A downloaded file is stored on your device.

Analytics and hosting

This version does not install advertising pixels, behavioural analytics or session-recording tools. The hosting service may process technical requests and, for a private review site, authentication information to deliver and protect access to the site.

Requests about an enquiry

Contact operations@digiator.com to ask about access, correction or deletion of information you have shared with Digiator. The team will consider the request and any applicable record-keeping obligations. This page does not promise a retention period that has not been established for the relevant records.

Changes

This information describes the functionality of this website version. It should be updated if hosted forms, analytics, email subscriptions or other data services are introduced. Last updated: 22 October 2026.
